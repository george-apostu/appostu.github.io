F.A.Q

A few things people usually ask

1. Which calendar does the app follow?

The Romanian Orthodox calendar: fixed red-cross and black-cross holidays, movable feasts, fasting periods, and saints of the day. It is a personal companion, not an official publication of the Church.

2. Is the app free?

Yes. Core calendar, saint of the day, and daily verse are free. Holy Cross Premium ($4.99/month, with a 7-day trial) unlocks unlimited journal entries, the full prayer library, fasting and attendance tracking, confession preparation, name days, badges, smart notifications, and an ad-free experience.

3. Where is my journal stored?

Journal entries, check-ins, fasting logs, and confession notes live on your device. If you sign in with Apple or Google, account data can sync with your login; see the Privacy Policy for details.

4. Does this replace a priest or parish?

No. The app is a reminder and a notebook. Confession, communion, and pastoral guidance belong with your parish priest.
